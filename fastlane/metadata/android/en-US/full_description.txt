NOTORYX Core turns your phone and a MeshCore LoRa radio into a field communicator that works without cell service, Wi-Fi or the internet. Messages hop radio to radio across the mesh.

Messaging
• Direct and channel messages over the mesh, with delivery status
• Search every saved conversation
• Read messages aloud, hands-free
• Area-limited channel messages that stay within a radius of you (needs flood-corridor firmware)

Trips and safety
• Plan a trip with up to six contacts
• Check in ("I'm OK", "At Camp", "Delayed", "Heading Back") and see who it reached, with a one-tap resend
• Check-in reminders, even when the app is closed
• Alerts when a trip contact goes quiet too long
• Hold-to-send SOS with your radio's position, repeating until someone replies
• Field Log of everything sent and received

Maps and coverage
• Live mesh map and offline map packs
• Coverage capture: record, resume and export field sessions
• Route and field intelligence

Your radios
• Connect over Bluetooth, USB or TCP
• Radio overview, stats, battery and LoRa settings
• Repeater and room server tools: login, status, neighbors, telemetry, command line and access control
• Live sensor updates when readings change
• Command line on your own radio and, with permission, a trusted contact's
• Battery saver

NOTORYX Core needs a radio running MeshCore firmware. Overdue alerts, check-ins and SOS depend on radio range and the mesh; they are not a replacement for an emergency service or satellite messenger.

NOTORYX Core is open source and builds on the MeshCore Open project.
